# P-05 — Reports with one shape

- **Status:** to discuss
- **Branch:** feat/… (to decide)
- **Upstream:** maybe; it changes upstream's reports, so it may stay ours

## Why

The user likes Securo's reports and finds Firefly's slow and too full. Securo has
three (net worth, income and expenses, cash flow) and they share one shape,
which is what keeps them light:

- one headline figure with its change against the previous period, in amount
  and percent, and a small breakdown under it;
- one trend (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly) over the last N months, the year
  to date or a custom range;
- one composition of the total, by account or by category;
- the same account filter on every report.

## To look at first

What Money's Reports page and Overview cards already do (comparison with the
previous period or last year, saved reports, CSV export), and how far they are
from that shape. Detail stays behind a tap, not on the first screen.
